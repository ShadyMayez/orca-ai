FAQ

What does Orca AI build?

Four things: automation solutions that take routine work off your team, AI integrations that connect models to the systems you already use, multi-step AI workflows, and AI agents and chatbots embedded across your website, WhatsApp, apps and internal tools.

Do we have to change the tools we use?

No. We build around your existing stack, whether that is a CRM, an ERP, Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, Shopify or a custom system with an API. The goal is less switching between tools, not one more.

Which AI models do you use?

We pick the model per job, from providers such as OpenAI, Anthropic and Google, and open models where privacy or cost calls for them. You are never locked into one provider.

How do you keep our data safe?

Agents run on your accounts and API keys, each with access only to what its job needs. Sensitive actions require approval, and we agree data handling in writing before any build begins.

Which languages can the agents handle?

Arabic, including Egyptian and Gulf dialects, English, and mixed Arabic-English messages, plus other languages on request. We tune the tone to match your brand.

How long does a project take?

It depends on the scope. We agree the timeline in the blueprint before building, and most projects start with a small pilot so you see results before a full rollout.

How does Orca AI charge?

Each build is a scoped project with a price agreed up front. Ongoing monitoring and improvement is a monthly plan sized to how much the agents do. Model and platform usage is billed at cost to your own accounts.

What happens when the AI is not sure?

It stops and hands over. You decide the rules: which topics, amounts or customers always go to a person, and the agent passes the full conversation so nobody has to ask twice.

Do you support what you build after launch?

Yes. We monitor the agents, review difficult cases, fix issues and add new skills as your business changes. You also get documentation, so your team understands what runs where.
